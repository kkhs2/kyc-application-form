Steps involved in your credit increase process

Please Note - When making an application for credit increase you’ll be asked to provide personal and business information which will be used as part of our decision making process.

Step 1. You will need to complete and sign an online application form. This will include personal and business information.

Step 2. Your application will be reviewed. This process may include the use of a credit reference agency to determine the level of credit that Booker can offer you.

Step 3. You will be notified in writing when your application is complete. You can also check back here on the progress and status of your application.

Data Processing

As part of your application we will submit your information to credit reference agencies to check your credit status and identity. They will provide us with information about your financial history which we will use as part of our decision making process, to determine the level of credit that Booker will offer you. By signing the application for credit, you are giving permission for Booker to complete a full credit review on both your company and you as an individual if you are a sole trader or have signed a personal guarantee. A record of this search will be recorded and can be seen by other companies if they carry out a credit enquiry, We will also check a number of criteria that include but are not limited to Public Records, Electoral Role, Previous Searches and other Credit Commitments

Should credit be refused, we recommend that you obtain a copy of your credit reference file from a Credit Reference Agency.
